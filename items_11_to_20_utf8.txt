RB 1 SSR 3 question: RB 1 SSR 3 states that Russian MMC must take a NMC after German reinforcements are setup. It states that a unit loses its concealment "only" if it loses its good order status. Does this mean that failure of the NMC only strips concealment or is the unit subject to breaking, ELR, casualty reduction also?
----------------------------------------
Ground Truth Answer:
RB 1 SSR 3 states that Russian MMC must take a NMC after German reinforcements are setup. It states that a unit loses its concealment "only" if it loses its good order status. Does this mean that failure of the NMC only strips concealment or is the unit subject to breaking, ELR, casualty reduction also? 

RB1 SSR3 "Each such unit loses its concealment (if any) only if the NMC causes it to lose Good Order status, regardless of enemy LOS."
Could be read as: ""Each such unit loses its concealment (if any) as the sole consequence if the NMC causes it to lose Good Order status, regardless of enemy LOS.",
or could be read as: ""Each such unit loses its concealment (if any) just in the case when the NMC causes it to lose Good Order status, regardless of enemy LOS."

I can see reading this two ways, as you suggest. In one way the "only" is attached to the loss of concealment clause, i.e. the sole consequence of losing its Good Order is to lose concealment. But that means that the sole consequence of failing the NMC is to lose concealment, i.e. not only does failing the NMC not HoB, ELR or casualty reduce, it also does not break. If that is the case then the unit never breaks (or goes berserk) and so always remains Good Order after the NMC and so never becomes unconcealed. That seems unlikely. So I have to conclude the intention was the second interpretation, i.e. the NMC causes all normal effects including breaking, HoB, ELR and Casualty MC. If after that MC it is no longer Good Order, then it loses concealment. So for instance if the result of the NMC is HoB with hero creation and battle hardening then the unit does not lose concealment, but if the result is HoB, berserk then it does lose concealment.

Title: Desperation Morale
Expected Rules: []
Query Intent: None | Concepts: ['movement_bypass', 'fire_attacks', 'morale_pin_rally', 'terrain_los', 'ordnance_guns', 'leaders_heroes']
Query:
Desperation Morale: What does "taking the possibility of cowering into account" mean in rule 10.62 ?
----------------------------------------
Ground Truth Answer:
What does "taking the possibility of cowering into account" mean in rule 10.62 ? 
Take all thirty-six possible DRs, from 1,1 to 6,6. Look up the result on the IFT for that FP (applying the DRM for the attack) for all the thirty-six DRs. If a particular DR is a doubles, and the attack might cower, use the result from the IFT column for the cower, not the original IFT column. At the end of the exercise you will have thirty-six possible results. If any of them is a NMC or better, the broken unit is marked with a DM.

As a practical matter it is sufficient to look at the result on a DR of three if the attack can cower or on a DR of two if the attack can't cower. If the result is a NMC or better, broken units will be marked DM by the attack.

In both cases this is regardless of the actual IFT DR.
