placed normally.

by Avalon Hill. They appear in Bruce Wigdor’s Internet Up Front (IUF) program.  (see

- Andy Maly’s errata has been incorporated into the scenarios.
-  Scenario J, Free for All, has been extended to allow more than 3 players.  If played with 3, it matches the
originally published scenario.   (Since Up Front is essentially a two player game, I have never played this
scenario and it has not been playtested.)
