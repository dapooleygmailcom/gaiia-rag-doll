----------------------------------------
Ground Truth Answer:
A23.5 "In order for a DC attack (as with any ordnance Direct Fire vs a Location containing an AFV in combination with unarmored targets) to affect an AFV, it must be predesignated as being made against the AFV."

A23.71 " A detonated Set DC is resolved on the 36+ FP column of the IFT (or with 18 FP [the 16 FP column] vs concealed units) with a -3 DRM and no TEM including vs the vulnerable PRC of an AFV (with +2 CE DRM)." 

A DC can't be declared as set against a vehicle (and it is unlikely that there would be an enemy vehicle in the Location when it is set anyway). The only effect will be general collateral vs. PRC and/or possible indirect effects vs. terrain.

An A-T Set DC can affect a vehicle [G1.6121].

Title: Does the "No Quarter" declaration allow use Low Crawl to units adjacent to the enemy?
Expected Rules: ['A10', 'A10.53', 'A10.531', 'A20', 'A20.21', 'A20.3', 'E1', 'E1.54']
Query Intent: None | Concepts: ['movement_bypass', 'morale_pin_rally', 'terrain_los', 'close_combat_melee', 'concealment_snipers', 'ordnance_guns', 'leaders_heroes', 'scenarios_setup']
Query:
Does the "No Quarter" declaration allow use Low Crawl to units adjacent to the enemy?: Hi all.

As stated in the question, I have a doubt regarding rules A20.21 and A20.3. If one side has declared No Quarter, can an enemy unit use Low Crawl if it is adjacent to that side at the start of its Rout Phase? Or does the prohibition under rule A20.21 still take precedence?

Thanks in advance.
----------------------------------------
Ground Truth Answer:
Why are you equating Low Crawl with being ADJACENT????

10.52 LOW CRAWL: Low Crawl is a rout of one Location which requires 
the entire MF allotment of the routing unit (but is still considered a form of 
Assault Movement should the routing unit enter a FFE Blast Area). A rout-
ing unit using Low Crawl cannot be Interdicted. Low Crawl cannot be used 
to enter Marsh or a Water Obstacle, or in any stream unless dry. Low Crawl 
cannot be used to exit an enemy-occupied Location [EXC: Night (E1.54)]. 
All other Rout provisions apply unchanged to Low Crawl, e.g., rout must still 
be towards the nearest woods/building Location within 6 MF.

Nothing in 10.52 requires a unit that wants to Low Crawl be ADJACENT to an enemy unit (Known or otherwise).

A Routing Unit has these options:
* If not subject to possible surrender, it designates its Rout target (nearest woods/building within 6 MF) and:
1) Routs using its 6 MF (3 for Wounded Leader, etc). towards that target; if moving into Open Ground it may be subject to Interdiction (A10.53; A10.531).
2) Declares Low Crawl and only Routs a single hex, AGAIN TOWARDS THE ROUT TARGET.

*If No Quarter has NOT been declared and it begins the Rout phase adjacent to a KEU and cannot Rout in any way to avoid Interdiction, it Surrenders to that adjacent KEU.

*If No Quarter HAS been declared and it begins the Rout phase adjacent to a KEU and cannot Rout in any way to avoid Interdiction, it does NOT Surrender and may either Rout using its 6 MF (taking the Interdiction MC(s)) or Low Crawl one hex and avoiding all Interdiction rolls.

Note that if the broken unit begins the Rout phase adjacent to a KEU, but has a VALID Rout path that does _not_ subject it to Interdiction, it does ** NOT ** Surrender and can Rout via the first bullet above (either using full 6 MF or Low Crawling one hex ... but both still must be towards it's designated Rout target).
